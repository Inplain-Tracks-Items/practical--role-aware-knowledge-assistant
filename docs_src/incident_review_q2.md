# Incident Review Q2 2026

Confidential. For the safety team and leadership. Scanned from the signed paper report.

## Summary

In the second quarter Northwind recorded 14 incidents: 9 minor vehicle damages, 3 cargo damages, 1 near miss at the Antwerp depot and 1 serious injury. The serious injury happened on 12 May at the Duisburg depot, when a forklift reversed into a warehouse worker during night loading. The worker returned to work after six weeks.

## Root causes

The forklift reversing alarm was disconnected. Night loading had no dedicated banksman, and the walkway markings at dock 7 were worn. Two of the three cargo damages were caused by load securing straps that were past their replacement date.

## Actions

All forklift alarms are now checked at the start of every shift. A banksman is mandatory for night loading at every depot from 1 July. Walkway markings at Duisburg were repainted in June. Straps older than 24 months are replaced, and strap age is added to the pre-trip inspection.

## Consequences

Because of the serious injury, the safety part of the 2026 leadership bonus is set to zero. The Duisburg depot manager follows a safety leadership course in the third quarter.
