# Customer Service Level Guide

This guide is for sales, dispatch and leadership. It explains the service levels Northwind sells and what happens when we miss them.

## Service tiers

Northwind sells three service tiers. Standard delivers within a four-hour window on the planned day. Priority delivers within a two-hour window and includes proactive delay alerts. Critical delivers within a one-hour window, with a dedicated vehicle when needed and a named dispatcher for the account.

Every customer contract names its tier per lane. The tier of a shipment is shown in the planner next to the shipment id.

## Penalties and credits

When Northwind misses a delivery window for a reason within its control, the customer receives a service credit: 5 percent of the shipment price for Standard, 10 percent for Priority and 20 percent for Critical. Credits are capped at 15 percent of the monthly invoice. Delays caused by the customer, by road closures or by extreme weather do not earn credits.

## Account management

Each customer has one account manager from sales. The account manager informs the customer about red shipments, handles complaints within one working day and reviews the service level with the customer every quarter. Discounts above 8 percent need approval from leadership.

## Quoting new lanes

Sales quotes new lanes with the pricing calculator. Quotes for Critical service need confirmation from dispatch that a dedicated vehicle is available. A quote is valid for 30 days.
