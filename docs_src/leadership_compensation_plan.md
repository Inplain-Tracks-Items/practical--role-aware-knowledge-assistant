# Leadership Compensation Plan 2026

Confidential. For the leadership team only. This plan describes how Northwind's directors and depot managers are paid in 2026.

## Base salary bands

Depot managers are paid between 78,000 and 96,000 euros per year. Directors are paid between 112,000 and 138,000 euros per year. Positions within the band are reviewed every January by the remuneration committee.

## Annual bonus

The annual leadership bonus is between 0 and 25 percent of base salary. It is calculated from three company targets: on-time delivery above 96 percent counts for 40 percent of the bonus, operating margin above 7.5 percent counts for 40 percent, and zero fatal or serious injury incidents counts for 20 percent. If a serious injury incident occurs, the safety part of the bonus is zero for the whole leadership team.

The bonus is paid in March after the annual accounts are approved.

## Long-term incentive

Directors receive a long-term incentive of 15,000 euros per year, vesting after three years of continued employment. A director who leaves before vesting loses the unvested amounts.

## Company car and benefits

Leadership members receive an electric company car with a lease budget of 900 euros per month, a phone and a pension contribution of 10 percent of base salary.

## Confidentiality

Compensation details in this plan may not be shared outside the leadership team and the remuneration committee.
