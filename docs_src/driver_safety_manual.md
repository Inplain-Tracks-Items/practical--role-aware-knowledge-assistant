# Driver Safety Manual

This manual is for Northwind drivers and the dispatchers and safety officers who plan and supervise their trips. It explains the rules that keep drivers, cargo and other road users safe.

## Driving and rest times

A driver may drive at most 9 hours per day, extended to 10 hours no more than twice a week. After 4.5 hours of driving a break of at least 45 minutes is mandatory; it may be split into 15 minutes followed by 30 minutes. Weekly driving time may not exceed 56 hours, and 90 hours over two consecutive weeks. The tachograph record is the only accepted proof of driving time.

Dispatch never plans a route that requires breaking these limits. If traffic makes a delivery window impossible within the limits, the driver stops and calls dispatch; the delivery is rescheduled.

## Pre-trip inspection

Before every trip the driver checks tyres, lights, brakes, mirrors, load securing and the fire extinguisher, and signs the inspection in the driver app. A vehicle that fails the inspection does not leave the depot until the workshop clears it.

## Incidents on the road

After any collision, near miss or cargo damage the driver secures the scene, calls emergency services when people are hurt and then calls the 24/7 safety line, extension 4400. The driver writes a short report in the driver app within two hours. Photos of the scene and the cargo are attached when it is safe to take them.

## Safe driving bonus

Drivers earn a safe driving bonus of 150 euros per quarter when they have no preventable incidents, no speeding alerts from the telematics system and complete inspection records for every trip. The bonus is paid with the salary of the first month after the quarter. Disputed alerts are reviewed by the safety team within ten working days.

## Hazardous goods

Only drivers with a valid ADR certificate may carry hazardous goods. The ADR documents travel in the cab, and the orange plates are uncovered only while hazardous goods are on board.
