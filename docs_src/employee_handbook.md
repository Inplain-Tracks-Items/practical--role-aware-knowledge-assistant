# Northwind Logistics Employee Handbook

Northwind Logistics is a regional freight carrier with depots in Rotterdam, Antwerp and Duisburg. This handbook applies to every employee: drivers, dispatchers, safety staff, sales and leadership. It describes how we work together and where to find help. Team-specific rules live in the manuals of each team.

## Working hours and time off

The standard office week is 38 hours, Monday to Friday. Drivers and dispatchers work in rotating shifts published two weeks in advance in the shift planner. Every employee receives 26 days of paid annual leave. Leave requests go through the HR portal at least ten working days before the first day off, except for emergencies.

Sick leave must be reported to your direct manager before the start of your shift. From the third consecutive day of absence a doctor's note is required.

## Pay days and expenses

Salaries are paid on the 25th of every month. Travel and meal expenses are reimbursed within 30 days when submitted with a receipt in the expense app. Fuel for company vehicles is paid with the fleet fuel card only; private fuel cards are never reimbursed.

## Conduct and communication

We treat customers, colleagues and road users with respect. Harassment, discrimination and working under the influence of alcohol or drugs lead to immediate suspension. Customer information is confidential and may only be shared with colleagues who need it for their work.

Company news is published every Monday in the Northwind Bulletin. Questions about this handbook go to hr@northwind.example.

## Safety for everyone

Safety comes before speed, for every role. Anyone who sees an unsafe situation in a depot or on the road may stop the work and report it to the safety team on the 24/7 safety line, extension 4400. Nobody is ever blamed for stopping work in good faith.

## Learning and development

Every employee has a yearly development budget of 750 euros for courses and certifications related to their role. Managers discuss development goals in the annual review in March.
