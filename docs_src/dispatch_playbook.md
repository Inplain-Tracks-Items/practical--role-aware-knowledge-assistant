# Dispatch Operations Playbook

This playbook is for dispatchers, the safety team and leadership. It describes how Northwind plans, monitors and recovers shipments every day.

## Daily planning cycle

Orders received before 14:00 are planned for the next day. At 15:00 the planning dispatcher assigns each shipment to a route, a vehicle and a driver, checking driving-time limits and ADR requirements. Plans are frozen at 18:00; later changes need the approval of the shift lead.

Each shipment has an id of the form NW-1234. The shipment id is used in every system: the planner, the driver app, the customer portal and invoices.

## Monitoring

During the day dispatchers follow every vehicle on the telematics map. A shipment is flagged amber when its estimated arrival is more than 30 minutes behind the delivery window and red when it is more than 90 minutes behind. Amber shipments are checked with the driver; red shipments are escalated to the shift lead and the customer is informed by the account manager.

## Recovery playbook

When a vehicle breaks down, dispatch first secures the driver's safety, then looks for the nearest Northwind vehicle with free capacity. If none is available within 60 minutes, a partner carrier from the approved list is booked. Every recovery is logged with its cause so that recurring problems reach the weekly operations review.

## Capacity and overtime

Overtime for drivers is offered in order of the overtime list and is never mandatory. Dispatchers do not ask a driver to extend a shift beyond the legal driving time, even when a customer escalates.

## Weekly operations review

Every Friday dispatch, safety and leadership review on-time delivery, recoveries, incidents and customer complaints. The target on-time delivery rate is 96 percent per depot.
